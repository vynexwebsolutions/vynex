Yes. We build Android apps, iOS apps, web apps and websites, and we can connect them all to the same system.

Yes. We integrate M-Pesa (the pay prompt on the customer's phone) and other payment methods into websites and apps.

Yes. We can give you a simple admin panel to change text, images and prices without touching any code.

Yes. We offer maintenance and technical support. Ask about support options when we speak.
